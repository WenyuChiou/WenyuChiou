## Research

My research asks when a simulated decision-maker can be trusted. I designed and fielded a 937-household flood-adaptation survey in New Jersey's Passaic River Basin, identified owner and renter decision pathways through structural equation modeling, and built a Bayesian-calibrated agent-based model of 52,141 households coupled to a catastrophe flood model with National Flood Insurance Program mechanics. With my advisor, I published Yang, Y. C. E., & Chiou, W. (2026). Leveraging Large Language Models for Agent-Based Simulation of Human–Water System Interactions. *Water Resources Research*, 62(6), e2025WR042111. DOI: [10.1029/2025WR042111](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025WR042111) — Published, second author of two. My current work tests whether LLM agents reproduce those empirically identified decision pathways.

The through-line: systems designed so that failure is visible — no silent passes.
